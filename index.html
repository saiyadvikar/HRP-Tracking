Clinical Delivery Outcome Definitions & Favourability Criteria
Scientific intrapartum classification framework for UHRP risk conditions
Objective Clinical Outcome Classification
Intrapartum delivery outcomes in the UHRP cohort are evaluated using standardized objective biomarkers (Status at Delivery from Form C) for core clinical risks and protocol-based facility appropriateness for obstetric/demographic risks.
1. Core Clinical Biomarker Conditions (From Form C Status at Delivery)
🩸 Severe Anemia (Hb < 7.0 g/dL) Hb Cutoff: 7.0 g/dL
Delivery outcome criteria:
Resolved (Hb > 7) Resolved: Severe anemia resolved (Hb > 7.0 g/dL at delivery).
Unresolved (Hb < 7) Unresolved: Persistent severe anemia (Hb < 7.0 g/dL at delivery).
🩺 Pregnancy-Induced Hypertension (PIH) BP Target: < 140/90 mmHg
Short-form statuses recorded at delivery:
Controlled (BP < 140/90) Favourable: Normotensive at delivery without eclampsia.
Uncontrolled (BP ≥ 140/90) Unfavourable: Persistent hypertension or severe-range BP.
🧬 Sickle Cell Disease (SCD) Target: Hb ≥ 7 & No Crisis
Short-form statuses recorded at delivery:
Stable (Hb ≥ 7, No Crisis) Favourable: Hemodynamically stable, no vaso-occlusive crisis.
Anaemic (Hb < 7) Unfavourable: Severe anemia (Hb < 7.0 g/dL) present at delivery.
Crisis at Delivery Unfavourable: Acute painful, hemolytic, or chest crisis.
🍬 Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM) Target: FBS < 95, RBS < 140
Short-form statuses recorded at delivery:
Controlled (Normal Glucose) Favourable: Euglycemia maintained (FBS < 95 & RBS < 140).
Uncontrolled (Hyperglycemia) Unfavourable: Blood glucose above targets at delivery.
2. Surgical, Obstetric History & Vulnerability Criteria
✂️ Previous LSCS (Uterine Scar Risk) Mode Classification
Clinical rationale based on intrapartum risk exposure:
Favourable (Elective LSCS) Favourable: Planned repeat cesarean avoids unmonitored scar dehiscence.
Unfavourable (Emergency LSCS) Unfavourable: Emergency presentation, failed labour trial, or acute fetal distress.
Favourable (Successful VBAC) Favourable: Monitored vaginal delivery completed without complications.
🏥 Previous Stillbirth / Neonatal Death & Teenage Pregnancy FRU Planning Protocol
Clinical protocol: CEmONC & 24x7 blood bank facility tier:
Favourable (Planned FRU) Favourable: Planned & delivered at First Referral Unit (DH / SDH / Tertiary / Private Hospital).
Unfavourable (Peripheral / Home) Unfavourable: Delivered at peripheral facility (CHC / PHC) or home without 24x7 C-section/SNCU.
Scientific Reporting Summary: When viewing the Case-by-Case table, each woman's specific risk factors are evaluated individually. Multi-risk patients display condition outcome badges for each registered risk category. Overall cohort favourability is synthesized in the KPI cards and Maternal Clinical Risk Resolution Segregated Bar.
